🎉 Open House — Free Sailboat Rides!

Once a month in summer, Cal Sailing Club throws open its doors to the public. Hundreds of people come down to the Berkeley Marina for free sailboat rides with our instructors, live music, food, and a party on the water. No membership needed — it's open to everyone, completely free.

How It Works

No prior experience needed. Learn at your own pace in a supportive community.

1

Join & Show Up

Join online or at the clubhouse. Sign-up takes just a few minutes. Members are usually happy to show you around anytime we're open.

2

Take Lessons

Learn from experienced volunteer members. Go at your own pace. Windsurfing lessons require sign-up. No reservation is needed for dinghy lessons — just drop in and put your name on the list.

3

Earn Ratings

Ratings unlock solo sailing, advanced boats, sailing or windsurfing outside of lesson times, and more.

4

Get Involved

Volunteer, teach, race, cruise, and make friends for life on the water.

Dinghy Ratings

Junior Dinghy Rating

The Junior rating is the second dinghy rating at CSC. It unlocks solo sailing across a wider area and is a prerequisite for keelboat sailing.

Prerequisite: Novice Rating Unlocks: Keelboat Sailing
Unrated Novice Junior Senior Cruising Senior Advanced Dinghies
Skills Required

What You Need to Know

Before requesting your Junior test, you should be comfortable with all of the following skills. Your instructor can help you practice any areas where you want more experience.

Boat Handling

  • →Rig and de-rig a dinghy independently
  • →Launch and recover a dinghy solo
  • →Sail on all points of sail (upwind, reaching, downwind)
  • →Tack and jibe reliably
  • →Sail small circles
  • →Slow sailing with good speed and directional control
  • →Depart from and return to the dock, in wind over 10 knots
  • →Crew overboard drill (retrieve a person or object from the water)
  • →Anchor the boat
  • →Navigate the Novice Area and Junior Area confidently

Safety & Rules

  • →Right a capsized dinghy and re-board
  • →Swim the boat back if becalmed
  • →Know and apply basic right-of-way rules
  • →Understand club boundaries and sailing area rules
  • →Recognize conditions when it is not safe to go out

Knowledge

  • →Parts of the boat and sail
  • →Points of sail and wind direction
  • →Basic weather awareness for the Bay
  • →How tides affect sailing conditions at CSC
How to Get This Rating

The Testing Process

There are three steps to earning your Junior rating. You can complete them in order as you progress through lessons.

1

Written Test

Covers sailing theory, right-of-way rules, and CSC policies. Taken online at any time — no appointment needed.

Study guide (PDF) Take online test
2

Rigging Test

Demonstrate that you can rig and de-rig a dinghy safely and correctly, independently. Completed at the club with a qualified evaluator.

Skills self-assessment Request rigging test
3

On-the-Water Test

Sail with a qualified evaluator who will observe your boat handling, safety awareness, and judgment on the water. Schedule this after passing your written and rigging tests.

Skills self-assessment Request on-water test
What This Rating Unlocks

Allowed Activities

With a Junior rating, you can do everything a Novice can do, plus:

The Junior Area: within sight of the clubhouse, north of Ashby Ave, east of the Emeryville Peninsula line. See Sailing Areas for the full chart.
🗺️

Expanded Sailing Area

Sail solo in the Junior Area (the full CSC sailing area), not just the Novice Area.

🕐

Sail Outside Lesson Times

Sign out a boat and sail independently any time the club is open, not just during scheduled lessons.

🚢

Keelboat Access

The Junior dinghy rating is a prerequisite for all keelboat sailing at CSC.

👥

Bring Guests

Take a guest out sailing as skipper (subject to club guest policies).

⛵

Advanced Dinghies

Become eligible to test on JY-15, Symmetric Spinnaker, and C420.

🛡️

Laser & Special Sign-Offs

Eligible for a special checkout to sail the Laser and UFO, with sign-off from a qualified member.

🏁

Racing

Participate in club racing events (written racing test also required).

Keelboat Ratings

Keelboat — Unrated

Keelboats are larger, more stable sailboats with enclosed cabins. Before earning a keelboat rating, you work through five skill levels under instructor supervision.

Prerequisite: Junior Dinghy Rating
Unrated Senior Cruising Senior Advanced Keelboats

How Keelboat Learning Works

Unlike dinghy sailing, keelboat learning is structured as five progressive skill levels. You work through each level with an instructor before becoming eligible to test for your Senior Keelboat rating. There is no separate "Unrated" test — the levels themselves are your pathway to the Senior rating.

Skills Required

The Five Skill Levels

Each level is signed off by a qualified instructor. You must complete all five before requesting your Senior Keelboat rating test.

L1

Dockside

Learn the parts of the keelboat, basic rigging, safety equipment, and dockside procedures. Completed before leaving the dock.

L2

Basic Sailing

Sail on all points of sail, tack, gybe, and handle the boat in open water under instructor supervision.

L3

Problems Outside

Handle unexpected situations on the water: engine failure, man overboard drills, weather changes, and emergency procedures.

L4

Sailing Close Quarters

Dock, undock, and maneuver the boat in tight spaces under power and sail. One of the most important practical skills.

L5

Intermediate Maneuvers

Spinnaker handling, anchoring, night sailing awareness, and more advanced boat handling skills.

How to Get Started

Process to Get This Rating

1

Written Test

Complete the keelboat intro written test before your first on-the-water lesson.

Study guide (PDF) Take online test
2

Sign Up for Keelboat Lessons

Sign up online for keelboat lessons. You'll work through Levels 1–5 with instructors over multiple sessions.

Sign up for lessons
3

Complete All Five Levels

Each level is signed off by your instructor as you demonstrate proficiency. Once all five are complete, you're eligible to test for your Senior Keelboat rating.

What You Can Do

Allowed Activities

While working through the five levels:

Sail in Lessons

Sail as crew or student on keelboats during scheduled lesson times with a qualified instructor.

Continue Dinghy Sailing

Your Junior dinghy rating remains active — continue sailing dinghies independently while working toward your keelboat rating. You may also attend dinghy cruises.

Track Your Progress

See which levels you've completed on your ratings dashboard. Each sign-off is recorded as you go.

Windsurfing Ratings

Senior Windsurfing Rating

The Senior rating is the highest standard windsurfing rating at CSC. It allows you to windsurf independently in all conditions, teach lessons, and take on leadership roles.

Prerequisite: Junior+ Rating Requires: Senior Project
Unrated Novice Junior Junior+ Senior
Skills Required

What You Need to Know

The Senior rating requires a high level of proficiency across all aspects of windsurfing. You should be comfortable in a wide range of wind and water conditions before testing.

On-the-Water Skills

  • →Waterstart in moderate to strong conditions
  • →Carve gybe reliably
  • →Sail confidently in 20+ knot winds
  • →Perform self-rescue in all conditions
  • →Demonstrate safe sailing judgment in challenging conditions

Equipment

  • →Rig any sail size independently and correctly
  • →Perform board repairs
  • →Diagnose and address common equipment issues
  • →Select appropriate equipment for given conditions

Teaching

  • →Demonstrate ability to teach beginner and intermediate windsurfers
  • →Explain techniques clearly and safely
  • →Manage student safety on the water
How to Get This Rating

The Testing Process

1

Senior Project

Propose and complete a project that benefits the club — teaching a series of lessons, repairing equipment, creating instructional materials, or similar. Requires ExComm approval before and after completion.

Propose a project
2

Written Test

Covers advanced windsurfing theory, safety, equipment, and teaching principles.

Study guide (PDF) Take online test
3

Senior Rigging Test

Demonstrate expert-level rigging of all sail sizes quickly and correctly.

Request test
4

On-the-Water Test

Sail with a qualified evaluator demonstrating your full range of skills including waterstart, carve gybe, and teaching ability.

Request test
5

ExComm Approval

Final approval by the Executive Committee after all tests and project are complete.

What This Rating Unlocks

Allowed Activities

All Conditions

Windsurf independently in the full range of Bay conditions, including high wind days.

Teach Lessons

Qualify as a windsurfing instructor and teach lessons to new and intermediate members.

Bring Guests

Take guests out windsurfing under your supervision.

Advanced Equipment

Access advanced windsurfing gear including smaller boards and larger sails.

Learn to sail on San Francisco Bay in our small, responsive dinghies. Lessons are drop-in, year-round, and taught by experienced volunteer members. No prior experience needed.

New to sailing?

Dinghy lessons at CSC are open to complete beginners and experienced sailors alike. You don't need to sign up in advance — just show up and add your name to the waiting list. Your first lesson will start with a brief orientation on shore before you get on the water.

What to Expect

Your First Lesson

1

Show up and sign in

Arrive at the clubhouse before the lesson starts. Sign in at the front desk and add your name to the waiting list. Have your member number ready.

2

Shore orientation

Your instructor will give a brief shore-side introduction covering safety, boat parts, and what you'll be doing on the water.

3

Get on the water

You'll sail with or alongside your instructor. Expect to capsize — it's part of learning, and you'll be shown how to right the boat.

4

Debrief and next steps

After the lesson, your instructor will give feedback and suggest what to focus on next time. Ask about ratings and what to work toward.

Dinghy

Starter: gloves, sunglasses strap, hat clip / hat with chin strap.

Bigger purchases: dinghy booties, polarized sunglasses, own PFD, own wetsuit, own Foulie.

Windsurfing

Starter: gloves, sunglasses strap, hat clip / hat with chin strap.

Bigger purchases: own wetsuit, harness, boots (TBD — confirm with windsurf instructors).

Keelboat

Builds on Dinghy gear — Junior Dinghy rating is a prerequisite.

Additional: dry bag, layers for longer/full-day trips, sun protection.

Expense Reimbursement

1. Get approval before making a purchase

Before spending money on club expenses, contact the ExComm member or chair or coordinator who can approve your purchase or provide a better alternative:

Category Contact
Kitchen supplies Banquet chair
Food for general club events/parties Vice Commodore, Banquet chair
Food for Fast Tracks, seminars, other teaching events Rear Commodore (sailing or windsurfing), Fast Track coordinator
Clubhouse or yard supplies, equipment, accessories Port Captain
Construction projects Commodore
Skiff maintenance supplies, gas, oil, etc. Port Captain
Dinghy parts, maintenance supplies, tools First Vice Commodore
Windsurf parts, maintenance supplies, tools Second Vice Commodore
Keelboat parts, maintenance supplies, tools Third Vice Commodore
Any of the above or other expenses Treasurer

The club has wholesale accounts or other purchasing arrangements that can be much less expensive and time-consuming than it would be for you to go to a store (even Costco). Contacting the Treasurer as well as the person(s) in charge will ensure that CSC gets the best deal and that you don't waste your time.

2. Get reimbursed

To get reimbursement for your approved purchases, email the treasurer at treasurer@cal-sailing.org and be sure to include:

  • Who approved it
  • Your PayPal, Venmo, or Zelle address for reimbursement (sorry, no checks or cash)
  • Pictures of your receipts (.jpg preferred) showing items and the total

Reimbursement usually takes a day or two, but sometimes the Treasurer falls asleep early, or something, so be ready to remind. Thanks!
